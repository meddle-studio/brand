# The magic trick

Read this first. Every other file in the kit is a specific application of this idea.

## Meddle is the status quo, interfered with.

Every Meddle surface is made of two forces:

1. **The status quo, done exceptionally.** Heavy Inter in ink on a quiet ground. A strict grid, hairline rules, small mono labels. Serious, disciplined, fundamentals nailed. This is the part a CEO trusts in a boardroom.
2. **One interference.** A single deliberate break in the pattern, and it's the part people remember. It's the thing that makes the work Meddle.

The trick is the ratio: **about 90% discipline, 10% meddling. One interference per surface.** Two interferences compete and neither lands. Zero, and it's just another competent studio.

## What counts as a surface

A **surface** is one composed view that's seen all at once: a headline, a slide, a social tile, an email, a business card, a poster, or **one viewport-height section of a web page**.

- **A web page is a sequence of surfaces.** Each section gets *at most* one interference, and most sections should have none. The page as a whole gets **one showpiece** (the hero video, or the wordmark at full bleed). In practice that's two or three interferences down a long page, and never two in the same viewport.
- **One move can do two jobs, and it counts once.** "Fast. Cheap. *And* Good." is both the wink and the italic. A headline and the aside directly under it that talks back to it ("*All three? At the same time? In THIS economy?*") are one move.
- **The signature doesn't count.** The sign-off *Fortune favors the daring.*, the corner wordmark, mono meta, arrows, and rules are present everywhere. They're the system, not an interference.

## The interference, in practice

The system already does this everywhere. These are the approved moves:

| Surface | The discipline | The interference |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | Heavy Inter, tight tracking | **One word or phrase in Libre Baskerville italic:** "Executive *Summary*", "It's time you seized an *unfair advantage*." |
| Sales copy | Plain claims, real prices | **One wink:** "Fast. Cheap. *And* Good." / "All three? At the same time? In THIS economy?" |
| Wordmark | Heavy, caps, tightly fitted | **Scale:** it runs edge to edge, bleeds off a business card, or fills a stage screen |
| Web page | Monochrome, gridded, calm | **Material:** one loop of glossy 3D glass, chrome, or liquid behind the type, with the wordmark flipped by `mix-blend-mode: difference` |
| Case study | Straight narrative | **One reframe:** "The category sells management. Armor Bands sells reclamation." |

## Why

Meddle's clients are challengers in serious categories: banks and credit unions, B2B SaaS, nonprofit finance, healthcare. They have to look *more* credible than the incumbents and *more* daring at the same time. The discipline earns the credibility. The interference earns the attention. Getting either one wrong means you lose the room, so the ratio matters more than either force on its own.

It's also the name. To meddle is to interfere with something that was going to happen anyway. Our promise is to help industry challengers interfere with the status quo, and the brand has to show that promise before it tells it.

## When the kit doesn't cover your case

1. Build the most rigorous, restrained version first: ink, grid, Inter, rules, mono labels. Make it good enough to stand on its own.
2. Then ask: *where is the one place this should break the pattern?* Pick the moment that carries the idea (the payoff word, the headline, the hero, the final CTA).
3. Break the pattern there, on purpose, at full volume. Leave everything else alone.
4. If you can't find the one place, the piece isn't clear enough yet. Fix the message before you decorate it.

## What the interference is not

- **Not decoration.** A flourish that doesn't carry meaning is noise. Italicize the payoff word, not a random one.
- **Not color.** The brand is monochrome. Purple is a signal (selection, focus), never the interference. Color comes from imagery.
- **Not chaos.** A stretched wordmark on a messy layout is just a mess. The interference only reads against discipline.
- **Not edginess for its own sake.** "Meddle" is mischievous, not hostile. We interfere with the *status quo*, not with the client, and never at anyone's expense.
