01 · Read firstbrand/magic_trick.md ↗

The status quo, interfered with.

Every Meddle surface has two forces: the status quo, done exceptionally (heavy Inter, ink, a strict grid, hairline rules, mono labels) and one interference, a single deliberate break that makes it Meddle.

About 90% discipline, 10% meddling. One interference per surface. Two compete and neither lands. Zero, and it's just another competent studio.

SurfaceThe disciplineThe interference
HeadlineHeavy Inter, tight trackingOne word in Libre Baskerville italic: "Executive Summary"
Sales copyPlain claims, real pricesOne wink: "Fast. Cheap. And Good."
WordmarkHeavy caps, tightly fittedScale: edge to edge, bleeding off the card, filling a stage
Web pageMonochrome, gridded, calmMaterial: one loop of glass, chrome, or liquid behind the type
Case studyStraight narrativeOne reframe: "The category sells management. Armor Bands sells reclamation."
