# Messaging

Approved lines you can reuse word for word. When a line here fits, use it instead of writing a new one: repeating the same lines is how a brand becomes recognizable. For prices and facts, use [`../facts.md`](../facts.md).

Lines marked **(live)** are in production on meddle.studio. Lines marked **(plan)** come from the business plan and are approved for any channel. Only the "Internal only" section of `facts.md` is private.

## Boilerplates

**Short (≈25 words): bios, directories, event programs**
> Meddle is a brand and design studio for industry challengers. By merging business strategy with exceptional design, we help companies reach their full potential. *Fortune favors the daring.*

**Medium (≈60 words): about sections, press, proposals**
> Meddle is a founder-led brand and design studio for industry challengers who are ready to interfere with the status quo. We merge business strategy with exceptional design to give ambitious companies an unfair advantage, specializing in brand strategy, verbal and visual identity, and websites. Clients work directly with senior talent, on a clear timeline, for a clear price.

**Long (≈110 words): RFPs, partner pages, the About slide**
> Meddle is a brand and design studio founded on a simple principle: challenge industry norms and interfere with the status quo. We specialize in high-impact brand strategy, visual identity, and website design for challengers ready to punch above their weight, with particular depth in financial services and B2B SaaS.
>
> Our key belief is that businesses don't buy "branding." They buy solutions to their problems. So we partner with clients to define and solve critical business problems, from growing deposits to clarifying complex offerings, while delivering bold, modern, sophisticated design that stands out in its category. Every engagement is founder-led and senior-only, with a transparent process and one clear price.

## Problem framings by segment

Use these to open sales conversations, deck sections, and landing pages for a segment.

- **Financial institutions:** "We need to grow deposits." → Brands that build trust, attract younger members and customers, and differentiate from legacy banks and neobank challengers. **(plan)**
- **B2B SaaS:** "We need more sales, but nobody understands what we do." → Clear brand narratives and digital experiences that articulate value, generate qualified leads, and empower sales teams. **(plan)**

## The process (sales)

1. **Intro Call** (30 min): establish fit and understand initial needs.
2. **Discovery Workshop** (90 min): a deep dive into the business, its challenges, and its goals.
3. **Formal Proposal:** detailed scope, timeline, and pricing.
4. **Contract & Deposit:** formalize the engagement and kick off. **(plan)**
