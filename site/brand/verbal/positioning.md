# Positioning

The strategic core. Read it before writing anything that explains who Meddle is, who it's for, or why someone should hire it. Specific numbers (prices, years, addresses) live in [`../facts.md`](../facts.md). Pull them from there so they only need updating in one place.

## One line

**Meddle is a brand and design studio for industry challengers.**

## Positioning statement

For **industry challengers**, meaning ambitious, maturing companies that are ready to take on the leaders in their category, **Meddle** is the **founder-led brand and design studio** that **merges business strategy with exceptional design** to give them an *unfair advantage*. Unlike traditional agencies, Meddle puts senior talent on every engagement, prices it transparently, and moves in weeks, not quarters.

## The promise

*It's time you seized an unfair advantage.*

We help companies reach their full potential by delivering **clarity of purpose alongside exceptional design.**

## The core belief

> Businesses don't buy "branding." They buy solutions to their problems.

This is the belief behind every other decision. We start with the business problem (grow deposits, win enterprise deals, earn a surgeon's referral) and design toward it. Craft is the tool, not the product. When copy drifts toward talking about aesthetics for their own sake, bring it back to the problem.

## Who we're for

**Industry challengers.** Companies that aren't afraid to interfere with the status quo in their category. They're usually past the scrappy stage and ready to punch above their weight.

Lead with *challengers*. That's the audience in every piece of public copy. Industry specifics come second, as proof of depth. Owner decision, 2026-09-30.

## Proof

Use real outcomes, never invented ones. Full stories live in the case studies on meddle.studio.

- **MFO Nonprofit Accounting.** A firm that grew on referrals, serving DC think tanks, biotech research organizations, and policy nonprofits with eight-figure budgets, had a brand that lagged its reputation. Meddle delivered a new visual identity and website. *"MFO had the reputation. Now they have the brand to match."*
- **Armor Bands.** A DTC ostomy-care company repositioned as "the performance lifestyle brand of ostomy care." After the rebrand, their first surgeon meeting at Ohio State ran 90 minutes against a 60-minute agenda, and the surgeon offered prime lobby placement.
- **Founder track record.** Creative Director and Head of Branding for fintech companies before founding Meddle. See `facts.md` for the canonical years of experience.

## Why Meddle (differentiators)

1. **Signature aesthetic.** Bold, clean, sophisticated work with a creative edge that makes clients stand out in conservative categories.
2. **Senior-level partnership.** Clients work directly with the founder from the first call to launch. Senior-only talent, one project focus, no hand-off to juniors, no bloat.
3. **Transparent, streamlined process.** A listen-first approach, a clearly defined project journey, and clear prices published up front. No guesswork. No haggling. No mystery pricing.
4. **Speed without shortcuts.** A complete visual identity in two weeks through Brand Sprints. "Whether moving fast or going deep, we do it right."

We also compete with other studios, freelancers, and in-house teams. Don't name or disparage any of them. The foil is the *model* (slow, bloated, opaque), never a specific company.

## Mission, vision, tagline

- **Mission:** Meddle is for those who aren't afraid to challenge their industries and interfere with the status quo. We exist to craft better brands and creative that inspire, enrich, and elevate the way people experience the world around them.
- **Vision:** A world where innovation, artistry, and meaning take center stage, where passion isn't sacrificed for profit or scale. We strive to be the go-to studio for industry challengers who understand that exceptional design is a fundamental business advantage.
- **Tagline / sign-off:** *Fortune favors the daring.*

## What Meddle is not

- **Not an agency.** We're a studio. "Agency" is the model we exist to replace. <!-- check-copy: ignore -->
- **Not a quick-fix shop.** We don't often take projects that need quick fixes or incremental improvements.
- **Not a production house.** Strategy leads. Production follows the strategic foundation.
- **Not rebellious for show.** MFO's clients didn't want a "rebellious" accountant, and Meddle didn't give them one. We make the client more daring *within* what their audience trusts.
