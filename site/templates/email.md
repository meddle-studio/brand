# Email recipe

**Read first:** `brand/verbal/voice-and-tone.md` (Email: human, brief, warm; no interference needed).

## Principles

- It's from Matt, so write in the first person, "I".
- **Short.** 3–6 sentences in the body. One clear ask.
- Specific to the recipient: reference their company, their problem, or what they said.
- No brand theatrics. The interference budget for a 1:1 email is zero. Confidence and clarity carry it.

## Signature

```
Matt Bacon
Founder & Creative Director, Meddle
meddle.studio
```
Plain text, or the small wordmark (`meddle-wordmark-sm.svg` exported to PNG at 2×, 30px tall). No quotes or banners.
