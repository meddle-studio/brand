    Why the baseline matters

    The version without the kit is good work, and that's the point. A capable agent makes reasonable, generic choices: an accent color, rounded cards, a flourish on every headline, a pitch in every post. None of it is wrong. None of it is Meddle. The kit replaces those guesses with decisions that have a reason on file, so the next page, deck, or post comes out on brand without anyone re-explaining the brand.
